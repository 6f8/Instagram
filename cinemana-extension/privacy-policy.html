Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Cinemana Download Button" Chrome Extension ("the Extension").

1. Data Collection

The Extension operates entirely locally within your web browser. We do not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal information, user data, browsing history, or download activity.

2. How the Extension Works

The Extension interacts with the webpage you are currently viewing (specifically the Cinemana Shabakaty domains) solely for the purpose of identifying video and subtitle file URLs so that you can download them directly to your device. This process happens dynamically on your local machine.

3. Permissions

The Extension requests the following permissions:

  • Downloads: To save video and subtitle files directly to your device's local storage.
  • Storage: To save your preferences locally within your browser.
  • Host Permissions (*://*.shabakaty.com/*, *://*.shabakaty.cc/*): To access and interact with the content on the Cinemana Shabakaty websites to find and facilitate the downloads.

4. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the Extension's practices, please contact the developer.
